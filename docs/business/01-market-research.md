# Market research — what was checked

Date of search: 2026-11-17. Search results only; no page was opened and checked in full, and no price below has been confirmed as current.

## Platforms and what was found

| Platform | Finding | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |

Listed starting prices for one seller: about ₩550,000 for a static-page build, ₩3.3M for a build with a database board, and ₩5.5M for feature-heavy builds. | Upwork | A few small-business Cloudflare Workers jobs, including one from a one-person antiques business asking for a Workers API integration with a possible usage dashboard. ## What this means

- Demand for a finished, small, remote "set up a personal business dashboard" service exists in principle (small admin/dashboard gigs are sold on Kmong), but the search found no evidence of volume or conversion rates for it.
- Prices vary widely. The offer's prices are therefore hypotheses to test with the first listing, not market-verified.
- Competition on Kmong for generic web builds is high. A narrower, clearly scoped offer for one-person businesses is the angle, and it is not proven.
- Upwork has a few small Cloudflare Workers jobs, but Upwork charges per connect to apply, and an account is needed. Those costs need approval.
